About

E. B. Wainwright writes clean fiction across two lanes: children’s stories in the Bumble. D. Bear universe, and grounded science-fiction that treats technology as intimate, practical, and morally complicated. The goal is simple: believable characters, real emotion, and prose that earns its moments.
